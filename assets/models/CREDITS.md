# 3D character models

Characters from **Ultimate Modular Men Pack** and **Ultimate Modular Women Pack** by **Quaternius**
(https://quaternius.com), released under **CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Converted from glTF to GLB for this app: unused animations removed, keyframes resampled,
smooth normals recomputed (the originals are flat-shaded; faces meeting at under 50° are blended,
sharper edges stay hard, eyes are left flat) and vertex data quantized (KHR_mesh_quantization).
Shapes, rigs and materials are otherwise unchanged.
